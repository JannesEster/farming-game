# 10 minute phone check

For Jannes. Use the live site on a phone, in Chrome or Safari:

https://jannesester.github.io/farming-game/

There is no account and no save. If the page looks stale, refresh it. The farm always opens on 1 August 2026, in the dry season, with the same layout.

## 1. Open it (about 1 minute)

The top bar shows a date in August 2026, Dry season, N$ 250,000, and a rainfall total in mm. You should see red sand, gold and green camps, camelthorn style trees, a dirt track, and a homestead with a house, a windmill, a round reservoir, and a kraal. A small group of cattle stands in one camp. A camp minimap sits in a corner.

## 2. Portrait feel (about 2 minutes)

Stay in portrait. Drag the land around. Pinch out until the whole fenced farm fits, then pinch back in until the cattle are easy to see. The top bar, the hint, and the minimap should stay usable and not cover the whole screen.

## 3. Landscape (about 1 minute)

Turn the phone sideways. Pan and pinch once. The buttons should still be easy to tap. Turn it back to portrait when you are done.

## 4. Select the herd (about 1 minute)

Tap the cattle. A card appears with the head count (120), a condition bar, the grass in the current camp, and how many days they have been in that camp. The camp they stand in is highlighted.

## 5. Move them (about 2 minutes)

Tap a different camp, either on the land or on the minimap. The cattle walk there. The card switches to that camp, and "days in this camp" goes back to 0. The grass percent should match the camp you can see, greener or more gold than the one you left.

Tap the homestead (the yard with the windmill). They should stay put, and the hint should say it is not a grazing camp.

## 6. Time and grass (about 3 minutes)

Tap Fast (the double arrow). Leave it for about 20 seconds. The date should race forward, the grass percent on the card should fall, and that camp should shift from green toward gold or brown. Other camps should not be eaten while the herd is away.

Tap Pause. The date should stop. Tap Play (single arrow). The date should move again, about one day a second.

If you have a couple of minutes more, leave Fast running until the date reaches November. The season label changes to Rainy season, and the rainfall total starts to climb on wet days. A camp you are not grazing can green up again. If you leave the herd on a brown camp until the grass line says there is nothing left, condition starts to fall. Move them to a greener camp and it can recover.

## Desktop, if you are at a computer

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click the cattle, then click a camp. Space pauses. F is fast. Keys 1, 2, and 3 are pause, play, and fast.

## If something is wrong

Note the phone, the browser, portrait or landscape, and what you tapped. A refresh puts the same farm back. Nothing is stored on the phone.
